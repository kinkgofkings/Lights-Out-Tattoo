1. Introduction

Lights Out Tattoo ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our web application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website, submit a tattoo booking inquiry, or interact with our integrated media.

2. Information We Collect

  • Client Booking Information: When you submit a booking inquiry, we collect your name, email address, phone number, tattoo placement, size preferences, and any optional reference images or notes you provide. This information is used solely to respond to your consultation and manage your appointment.
  • Technical Data: Standard server logs such as browser type, device information, and approximate location (to calculate proximity to our Winchester, VA studio) to ensure optimal website performance.

3. TikTok API & Social Media Data

Our website utilizes the official TikTok Open API to authenticate the studio owner and retrieve public tattoo showcase videos published by artist Tex.

  • No End-User TikTok Data Harvested: Website visitors do NOT log in with TikTok to browse our studio portfolio. We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal data from visitors related to their personal TikTok accounts.
  • Owner Authentication: The TikTok API authentication is used exclusively by the studio owner (Tex) to authorize synchronization of public video reels to the studio's portfolio section.
  • Data Sharing & Sale: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal data or TikTok API data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Storage & Security

All communication is transmitted securely over HTTPS with SSL/TLS encryption. We implement standard administrative and technical measures to protect against unauthorized access to submitted booking inquiries.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal booking inquiry records at any time. To request deletion of your information, simply email us at lightsouttattootex@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". All requested records will be permanently removed within 3 business days.
